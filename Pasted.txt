Build a Complete Enterprise AI Agent & Workflow Automation Platform

1. Your Role

Act as a principal AI engineer, senior full-stack architect, security engineer, and DevOps engineer.

Build an end-to-end, production-oriented enterprise AI platform named:

EnterpriseFlow AI — Secure Enterprise Agent & Workflow Automation Platform

The system must be a functional application, not a mockup, wireframe, or collection of disconnected demonstrations.

Implement the frontend, backend, database, AI services, agent workflows, document-processing system, authentication, authorization, testing, observability, infrastructure, and deployment configuration.

The project must demonstrate strong engineering practices suitable for a senior AI Application Engineer portfolio.

Do not stop after creating a project skeleton. Implement each feature with working code, integrations, validation, and tests.

Do not invent API responses, silently simulate successful operations, or claim something works without testing it.

2. Business Context

A real estate marketplace processes information from multiple sources and supports internal departments such as Finance, Human Resources, Legal, and Operations.

These departments deal with repetitive workflows, complex documentation, confidential information, and approval processes.

The company wants to introduce secure AI-powered agents that can automate routine tasks while maintaining strict control over sensitive information.

Build a platform that allows employees to:

* Interact with department-specific AI assistants.
* Search internal knowledge using RAG.
* Upload and process business documents.
* Automate repetitive workflows.
* Review AI-generated results.
* Approve or reject sensitive actions.
* Monitor workflow execution.
* Access information only within their authorized department.
* View audit histories.
* Manage agent configurations.
* Evaluate AI reliability and operational performance.

The platform must be extensible so additional business departments and AI agents can be introduced without redesigning the architecture.
