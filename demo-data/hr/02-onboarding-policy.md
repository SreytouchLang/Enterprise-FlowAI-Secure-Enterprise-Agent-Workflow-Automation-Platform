# Onboarding Policy

**Synthetic demonstration policy — no actual employment terms.**

Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

The manager assigns an onboarding buddy before the start date. New starters complete identity verification, security training, device setup, and benefits orientation during their first five business days. The manager reviews the checklist on day ten. Sensitive identity documents belong in restricted HR storage.
